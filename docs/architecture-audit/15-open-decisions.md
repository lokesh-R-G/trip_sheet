# Open Decisions & Technical Clarifications

This document compiles all business, legal, and technical questions that cannot be resolved solely from the source code, outlining their impact, default recommendation, and blocking status.

---

## 1. Inventory of Open Decisions

| # | Domain | Open Question / Ambiguity | Why It Matters | Recommended Default | Blocks Implementation? |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D1** | **Payroll** | **Trip Bata Calculation Formula**: Is Bata calculated as a flat amount per trip (e.g. ₹3,500), per kilometer driven (e.g. ₹2.50/km), or per calendar transit day? | Directly dictates the formula in `generatePayrollForMonth`. | Implement Bata as a configurable calculation type on `salary_components` (`fixed_per_trip`, `rate_per_km`, or `fixed_monthly`), defaulting to fixed amount per completed trip. | **Blocks Phase 10 (Payroll)** |
| **D2** | **Payroll** | **Advance Recovery Cap Policy**: Should authorized advance recovery be capped at a specific percentage of gross earnings (e.g. 30% or 50%), or can the company recover 100% of available net pay? | Protects drivers from zero-pay checks while recovering corporate funds. | Enforce a soft warning at 30% and a hard cap at 50% of gross earnings, configurable in `system_settings`. | **Blocks Phase 10 (Payroll)** |
| **D3** | **Payroll** | **Statutory Deductions (PF / ESI / Professional Tax)**: What are the exact percentage formulas and wage ceiling limits for provident fund, ESI, and statutory welfare? | Affects legal compliance of the generated payslips. | Model statutory deductions as standard `SalaryComponent` entries (`type: 'deduction'`, `calculationType: 'percentage'`) rather than hardcoded logic. | **No** (Configurable in schema) |
| **D4** | **Fuel Deductions** | **Deduction Valuation Tariff**: Should excess fuel consumption be billed at the global default fuel price, the weighted average price of bunkering top-ups for that vehicle, or the pump price on the specific trip slip? | Affects financial fairness of penalties imposed on drivers. | Use the weighted average price of fuel bunkered by the vehicle during that calendar month, falling back to `defaultFuelPricePerLitre`. | **Blocks Phase 9 (Fuel Deductions)** |
| **D5** | **Fuel Deductions** | **Excess Tolerance Policy**: Is the tolerance percentage (e.g. 5%) applied on top of the expected fuel volume or deducted from the penalty? | Formula: `Allowed = Expected * (1 + tol)` vs `Penalty = Max(0, Actual - Expected) * (1 - tol)`. | Retain observed formula: `Allowed = Expected * (1 + tol/100)`, excess is any fuel above `Allowed`. | **No** (Formula already coded) |
| **D6** | **Odometer** | **Tolerance on Rollback / Backward Jump**: The code allows a 5 km backward jump (`previousVehicleOdometer - startingReading > 5`). Is this acceptable to account for yard movements? | Determines whether yard maneuvering triggers a fraud/rollback discrepancy hold. | Retain 5 km yard buffer as a configurable setting (`odometerYardMovementBufferKm`). | **No** (Already implemented) |
| **D7** | **TLCM Pro** | **API Authentication & Contract**: What is the authentication scheme (Bearer JWT vs. API Key) and exact payload schema for the TLCM Pro API? | Required to write the external HTTP client in `src/modules/integrations/`. | Model after standard REST API (`POST /api/v1/vehicles/:id/attribute-tyre-distance` with Bearer token) and confirm with TLCM Pro team before Phase 11. | **Blocks Phase 11 (TLCM Pro)** |
| **D8** | **TLCM Pro** | **Tyre Installation Granularity**: Does TLCM Pro require sending individual tyre serial numbers with distance attribution, or does passing `vehicleId` + `acceptedDistance` cause TLCM Pro to attribute wear to all active fitted tyres automatically? | Changes whether Trip Sheet must track tyre positions locally or simply forward vehicle-level distance. | Pass `vehicleId` + `acceptedDistance` + `excludeStepney: true`, leaving axle wheel attribution to TLCM Pro. | **Blocks Phase 11 (TLCM Pro)** |
| **D9** | **Advances** | **Multi-Advance Trip Settlement Priority**: When multiple advances exist for a driver, which advance is settled first when trip expenses are approved? (FIFO vs. Explicit Association). | Dictates advance status updates (`partially_settled` vs `settled`). | Use explicit trip association (`advance.tripId`). For unassigned advances, settle via FIFO (First-In, First-Out by `advanceDate`). | **Blocks Phase 4/8** |
| **D10**| **Security** | **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: Will the system use internal username/password accounts or integrate with corporate Google Workspace / Azure AD SSO? | Determines user management and authentication service implementation. | Start with internal JWT + bcrypt username/password with support for OAuth2 SSO in Phase 12. | **No** (Internal auth sufficient for initial rollout) |
